Load the ingest's proposed T6 (t6_project_lines_proposed.tsv from the ingest output folder). Nothing is fixed here: the next step is yours to confirm.

Step 2: confirm the vendor chart of accounts

Schema 11 v1.1: ingestion proposes, the analyst confirms. Each vendor line appears verbatim with its proposed code, confidence colour-coded, and a basis field that cannot stay blank. Unmapped lines BLOCK generation. Splits are entered as code:share:basis pairs separated by a bar; children rows are created on export and carry the R108 flag until confirmed against better data.
